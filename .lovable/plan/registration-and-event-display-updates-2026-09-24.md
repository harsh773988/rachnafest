# Registration and event display updates

## What will change

- Remove the visible Lovable badge/logo from the public site while retaining required internal services.
- Make event cards more compact on the home and events pages; the large home image will remain unchanged.
- Add the supplied coordinator names and WhatsApp numbers for each matching event.
- After a public registration is successfully stored, show a confirmation with one WhatsApp button per available coordinator.
- Let the participant choose a coordinator; clicking sends a prefilled message containing the event, team name when applicable, and every participant’s submitted details.
- Keep registrations stored exactly as they are now so they continue appearing in the admin panel.
- Do not open WhatsApp for registrations entered by an administrator.

## Coordinator handling

- Only coordinators with a supplied WhatsApp number will receive a button.
- The duplicate “Dance Competition” labels beneath BGMI and Free Fire will be treated as a second coordinator for those cup events: Mr. Sagar Bauri.
- Incomplete blank coordinator rows will be omitted.
- Coordinator details will be kept in a central event-to-coordinator list so registration pages use the correct contacts.
